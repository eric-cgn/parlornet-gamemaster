ParlorNet Rulebooks 1.5

These are rulebooks for GameMaster 1.0.0, the Macintosh game program of 1991. GameMaster itself is not included.

ParlorNet signs you in to a ParlorNet parlor. From its window you start a game against the bot or another player, take the invitations other players send you, and take up the games you are already playing.

The game rulebooks play these games on a parlor, against the bot or another player. Checkers, Mancala and Five in a Row also play between two Macs with the same rulebook.

Checkers plays by American rules.
Mancala plays by Kalah rules with four seeds a pit.
Five in a Row plays on a 15 by 15 board, and five or more in a line wins.
Grid Words is the crossword game, and it plays only on a parlor. The parlor deals the tiles and checks the words, and it sends the board and the letter values of the table's own rules.
Rummy Tile is tile rummy for two by the standard rules. It plays only on a parlor, which deals the tiles and checks every turn.
Cannon Fire has two tanks shelling each other across a landscape. It plays only on a parlor, which flies the shells.
Backgammon plays by the standard rules with the doubling cube. It plays only on a parlor, which rolls the dice and checks every turn.

Click Continue. Install Location should name the folder that holds GameMaster; if it does not, click Choose... and pick that folder. The rulebooks go in that folder, next to GameMaster. Then click Install, open GameMaster and choose File > New Game.

GameMaster's own rulebooks stay as they are. Othello, Dots&Boxes and BattleShips play on a parlor with GameMaster's own rulebooks of those names.
